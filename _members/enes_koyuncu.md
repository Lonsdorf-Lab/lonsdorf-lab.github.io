Enes joined our team in Oct. 2026 as a student assistant supporting the [fearbase](fearbase.org) [project](https://lonsdorf-lab.github.io/projects/fearbase/). Within the project, his main tasks include the preparation and integration of experimental human fear conditioning datasets into the database as well as conceptual work on the project website.  

He is currently pursuing a Master's Degree in [Behavior: From Neural Mechanisms to Evolution](https://www.uni-bielefeld.de/fakultaeten/biologie/studium/studieninteressierte/master/bfnme/) at [Bielefeld University](www.uni-bielefeld.de).  His research interests focus on heart–brain interactions, interoception, and predictive processing, drawing from experiences at the Cognitive Neuroscience Lab at Bielefeld University as well as the Psychophysiology Lab at Acıbadem University.
