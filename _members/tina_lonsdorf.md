Tina's academic journey began with studies in Psychology at the [University of Trier](https://www.uni-trier.de/) and [Justus Liebig University Giessen](https://www.uni-giessen.de/) with a minor in Medicine, followed by a PhD in Clinical Neuroscience at [Karolinska Institutet](https://ki.se/en) in Stockholm.
She then moved to the [University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf](https://www.uke.de/), where she worked as a postdoc and later led her own research group within the Collaborative Research Center CRC 58 “Fear, Anxiety, Anxiety Disorders”.
In 2013, Tina established the [Emmy-Noether](https://www.dfg.de/en/research-funding/funding-opportunities/programmes/individual/emmy-noether) Research Group "[Fear Profiles](https://gepris.dfg.de/gepris/projekt/405663121?language=en)", funded by the [German Research Foundation (DFG)](https://www.dfg.de/en). Since 2023, she has been Professor of [Biological Psychology and Cognitive Neuroscience](https://www.uni-bielefeld.de/fakultaeten/psychologie/abteilung/arbeitseinheiten/14/) at Bielefeld University, while continuing her affiliation with the University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf as a guest researcher.
Tina is also actively involved in several collaborative initiatives: she’s a PI in the RTG "[Emotional Learning and Memory](https://www.emotionalmemory.de/)" at the [University of Hamburg](https://www.uni-hamburg.de/) and serves on the program committee of the DFG Priority Program "[META-REP](http://www.meta-rep.uni-muenchen.de/)". In addition, she chairs the [Division of Biological Psychology and Neuropsychology](https://www.dgps.de/fachgruppen/fgbi/) within the [German Psychological Society (DGPs)](https://www.dgps.de/).
