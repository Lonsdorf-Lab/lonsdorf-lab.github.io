Celine studied Psychology at [KU Leuven](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/) and completed her PhD in Clinical Psychiatry, investigating threat learning processes in adolescents exposed to childhood adversity. She then worked as a postdoctoral researcher at the [Laboratory for Biological Psychology (KU Leuven)](https://www.kuleuven.be/wieiswie/en/unit/regional/50320219), where she examined the mechanisms underlying the association between poverty and psychopathology. Celine was awarded a [Marie Skłodowska‑Curie postdoctoral fellowship](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships) to continue her work on threat and reward learning in adolescents exposed to adversity, which she will pursue at [Lonsdorf Lab](https://lonsdorf-lab.github.io/) starting February 2027.
