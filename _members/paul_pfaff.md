Paul studied Psychology at the Universities of Jena and Bochum. After that, he completed his Research Master at the [University of Amsterdam](https://www.uva.nl/en), focusing on experimental psychopathology and the processes underlying anxiety disorders. He is an incoming PhD student joining the at the [Institute of Systems Neuroscience](https://www.uke.de/english/departments-institutes/institutes/systems-neuroscience/index.html) ([University Medical Center in Hamburg-Eppendorf](https://www.uke.de/english/index.html)), where he will look into the effects of early adversity on fear and reward learning, including physiological and neural processes.
