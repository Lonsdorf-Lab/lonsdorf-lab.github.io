Maria joined the team in February 2023 to work as a PhD in the [FEAR-REP](https://gepris.dfg.de/gepris/projekt/501533638) project funded by the [German Research Foundation (DFG)](http://www.dfg.de/) where she is investigating replicability within the field of fear conditioning taking into account methodological, procedural and analytical heterogeneity. Maria holds a bachelor’s degree in [Philosophy-Neuroscience-Cognition](https://www.ovgu.de/unimagdeburg/en/Study/Study+Programmes/Master/Philosophy+_+Neurosciences+_+Cognition-p-23156.html) from the [Otto-von-Guericke University Magdeburg](https://www.ovgu.de/) and did her masters in [Cognitive and Integrative System Neuroscience](https://www.uni-marburg.de/en/studying/after-your-first-degree/masters-programs/degree-programs/kisneuroms) at the [Philipps University](https://www.uni-marburg.de/en) in Marburg.
