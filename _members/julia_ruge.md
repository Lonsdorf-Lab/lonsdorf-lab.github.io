Julia studied Psychology at the [Georg-August-University Göttingen](https://www.uni-goettingen.de/) and joined the team in September 2022 as a PhD student within the [Research Training Group 2753 "Emotional Learning and Memory"](https://www.emotionalmemory.de/). Her PhD project focuses on the associations between early adversity and fear conditioning, including underlying neural processes. Julia is part of the Hamburg team and the [Institute of Systems Neuroscience](https://www.uke.de/english/departments-institutes/institutes/systems-neuroscience/index.html) at the [University Medical Center in Hamburg-Eppendorf (UKE)](https://www.uke.de/english/index.html).
