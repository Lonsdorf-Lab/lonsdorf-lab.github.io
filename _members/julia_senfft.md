Julia is currently a Bachelor's Student in Psychology at Bielefeld University. She joined the Lonsdorf Lab in September 2025 as a research assistant, supporting [Dr. Zika](https://lonsdorf-lab.github.io/members/ondrej_zika.html)'s Task Ambiguity (TAMBI) project on learning under uncertainty and aversive threat. Her main responsibilities include assisting with the development and analysis of the TAMBI paradigm, as well as conducting data collection, and contributing to data processing and visualization in other ongoing lab projects.
