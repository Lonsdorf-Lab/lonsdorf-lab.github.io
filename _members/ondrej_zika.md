Ondřej has completed his PhD at [Oxford Univeristy](https://www.ox.ac.uk/) during which he investigated computational and neural mechanisms of aversive learning in humans. He then moved on to a PostDoc role at the [Max Planck Institute for Human Development](https://www.mpib-berlin.mpg.de/en) in Berlin, where he worked on cognitive mechanisms of learning under uncertainty in mental health conditions such as anxiety or eating disorders. After holding a dual position between Bielefeld University and [Karolinska Institute](https://ki.se/en), Sweden, Ondřej was offered an assistant professorship at the [University College Dublin](https://www.ucd.ie/), Ireland. His main research interests focus on understanding how we navigate and tolerate uncertainty in everyday decisions, and how inability to tolerate uncertainty can drive psychopathology.
