Conrad joined our team as a lab manager in September 2025. Hell will be responsible for maintaining and developing the lab infrastructure, as well as establishing a central hub for coordinating lab resources and the technical realization of research projects.

Conrad completed his master's degree in psychology at Bielefeld University after initial studies in medicine and biochemistry. A tinkerer from early on, he developed a passion for the technical aspects of experimental psychology. Prior to joining our team, he worked as a research assistant at Bielefeld University on a DFG-funded project on gaze perception.
