Part of our team attended this year's [Psychology and the Brain](https://www.dgps.de/fachgruppen/fgbi/news-details/retreat2025/) (Psychologie und Gehirn) conference in Heidelberg.

The conference was organised in collaboration with the [bioDGPs](http://www.dgps.de/fachgruppen/fgbi) and [DGPA](https://www.dgpa.de/) and featured a diverse programme covering a wide range of topics.

Our team contributed to the conference by presenting posters and giving talks on various subjects.
