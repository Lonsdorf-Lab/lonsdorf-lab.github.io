fearbase hackathon

We did it!

Today the fearbase team hosted its first fearbase hackathon. Following an intensive internal testing phase we put the first fully operational fearbase iteration to the test. Participants from all over Europe joined for a short overview of the project and thorough hands-on by testing the fearbase submission interface with datasets of their own.

The fearbase team says “Thanks!” to all those who participated.

Your feedback is invaluable developing the fearbase into a tool that meets your and all future fearbase users’ demands.
