Bio

Alex joined the team as a PhD student after studying Cognitive Science at University of Osnabrück. He is currently a post-doctoral researcher at Dr. Michael Gaebler’s “Mind-Body-Emotion” research group in the Department of Neurology at the Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences.

Projects
